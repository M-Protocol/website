How a PIN hides your apps

One code, and your apps are gone

Not just your photos: whole apps, like a messenger, can disappear from your phone and come back later with everything still in them. Here is exactly what happens.

  1. 1

    Put apps in the hidden space

    From your vault, move apps such as a messenger or gallery into a separate, private space on the phone. They keep working normally.

  2. 2

    Type your Lockdown PIN

    When you need to, type it into the disguise, just like a normal number. Nothing on screen gives it away.

  3. 3

    They vanish

    The apps disappear: no icon, no notifications, not even in search. The vault locks, and your main PIN stops working too.

  4. 4

    Bring them back

    When you are safe, your recovery passphrase restores everything, exactly as it was. Nothing is deleted.

What happens behind the scenes

The hidden space is an Android work profile: a separate, encrypted area of the phone, made to look like an ordinary work profile. Lockdown tells Android to freeze it. A frozen app can't run, show an icon or send notifications. Its data stays on the phone, encrypted.

Good to know

This feature is still experimental. It works best on Google Pixel. Some phone makers limit work profiles, so on other phones test it first. A forensic expert with special tools can see that a hidden space exists, but can't read what's inside.

Ghost VPN

Ghost Vault protects what's on your phone. Ghost VPN protects what leaves it.

Ghost VPN protects all your phone's internet traffic. It's a separate app that sends everything your apps do online through an encrypted tunnel to our own servers, with one tap.

  • Your whole phone

    Every app's internet traffic goes through one encrypted tunnel, built on WireGuard, a modern and well-studied VPN technology.

  • No activity logs

    Our servers don't record the sites you visit, your real internet address or when you connect. Your traffic passes through and is not stored.

  • No email, no name

    Your account is a random 16-digit number. We never ask for your name, email or phone number, and you can delete your account at any time.

  • Kill switch and private lookups

    If the tunnel drops, Android can block the internet so nothing slips out. Website lookups stay inside the tunnel.

  • Built to be checked

    Licensed as open-source software (GPL), with no homemade encryption and no tracking or analytics built in. Built to be audited.

What a VPN does

  • Hides the sites you visit from your Wi-Fi owner and your internet or mobile provider
  • Hides your real internet address from the websites you visit
  • Protects you on public Wi-Fi in cafés, hotels and airports

What it can't do

  • While you're connected, our servers carry your traffic. Every VPN works this way; we don't store it.
  • It doesn't make you anonymous. Sites you log in to still know it's you.
  • It can't help if the phone itself has spy software. For that, see the Privacy Checkup and the Ghost Phone.

Ghost Chat

Talk freely. No phone number, no name.

Ghost Chat is a private messenger for Android. Messages are end-to-end encrypted with the Signal protocol, so our server can't read them, and people know you only by an anonymous Ghost ID.

  • Signal-protocol encryption

    Built on libsignal, the library the Signal app uses, with post-quantum protection when a chat starts. No homemade cryptography.

  • An anonymous Ghost ID

    Share a short code like K7M2Q-X9D4R instead of your phone number. No name, email or phone number needed.

  • Chats stay on your phone

    Stored encrypted on your phone. Our server only holds encrypted messages until they're delivered, then deletes them.

  • Disappearing messages

    Set messages to vanish after 30 seconds to a week. Compare safety numbers to be sure you're talking to the right person.

  • Quiet by design

    Screenshots are blocked, notifications only say "New message", and there are no Google services, ads or analytics.

What Ghost Chat does

  • Keeps what you write between you and the person you write to
  • Keeps your phone number out of it
  • Lets strangers reach you only as a message request you can accept or block

What it can't do yet

  • For now our server can see which Ghost IDs message each other, though never what they say. Hiding this ("sealed sender") is the next step.
  • The server sees when your phone is online and its internet address. Ghost VPN hides that.
  • It can't stop someone photographing their screen, and chats aren't backed up: a lost phone means lost chats.
  • It hasn't been audited by outside experts yet.

Honest limits

What it can't protect you from

No app is unbreakable, and we will never claim this one is. Knowing the limits keeps you safer.

A phone that is already compromised

If the phone is rooted, runs a modified system, or has spyware that can see the screen while the vault is open, that software can see what you see. The Privacy Checkup looks for signs of this but can't promise to find everything.

Someone watching you type

If someone sees you type your PIN, they know it. Decoy and Lockdown PINs help here, but only if you use them.

Copies stored elsewhere

Cloud backups, photos already uploaded, chat backups or files sent to others are outside the phone. No wipe can erase them.

A forensic examiner

An expert with an unlocked phone and special tools can see that the app and the hidden apps space exist, even though the contents stay encrypted.

Phone calls and the real lock screen

Calls from the vault are normal calls, so your phone company has a record. And no app can read what you type on the phone's own lock screen; the app can only count failed unlocks.

Not yet independently reviewed

The app has automated tests and internal security checks. An outside security review is planned before sale. Hidden apps and some lock-screen features have so far been tested only on emulators.

You are responsible for how you use the app and for following the laws where you are. Our apps are made to protect people against theft, snooping, spy apps and abuse, not to hide evidence of crimes.

What happens if I forget my PIN?

Your recovery passphrase (12 characters or more, chosen at setup) brings back access. Keep it written down somewhere away from the phone. Without the PIN and the passphrase, nobody can open the vault, including us.

Can you see my data or reset my PIN?

No. The app never connects to the internet and there are no accounts. Everything stays encrypted on your phone. We will never ask for your PINs or passphrase, and you should never share them.

What if someone finds the app and tells me to open it?

Use a decoy PIN to open a believable fake vault, or your Lockdown PIN to quietly hide everything. Nothing on screen says which PIN you typed. Practise first with Practice mode so it feels natural.

Does it work on my phone?

Any Android phone with Android 8.0 or newer. It works best on Google Pixel, then Samsung. Some makers, such as Xiaomi, Honor and Huawei, limit apps running in the background; the app walks you through the extra settings. iPhone is not supported.

Why does it ask for permissions like Accessibility?

Nothing is asked at install. Each permission is requested only when you turn on the feature that needs it (for example, the app locker needs Accessibility), with a screen explaining what it is for and what the app never does with it. Every one is optional.

Will the wipe PIN delete everything?

Only if you turn it on. You choose either a full factory reset of the phone, or only this app's data and hidden apps. It cannot be undone, and it doesn't reach copies in cloud backups.

Should I buy the Ghost Phone or a licence?

If your safety depends on it, choose the Ghost Phone: it is set up and hardened for you, on the phone Ghost Vault works best on. A licence suits people who already have a recent Android phone, ideally a Pixel, and are comfortable installing an app from a file themselves.

Is there a VPN?

Ghost VPN is a separate app; ask us about it. Ghost Vault itself never goes online and never uses the VPN. Ghost VPN protects what your other apps do on the internet. See what it does and doesn't do.

How is Ghost Chat different from other messengers?

It uses the same well-studied Signal protocol for encryption, but you sign up without a phone number, name or email: people know you only by your Ghost ID. It uses no Google services. For now our server can see who messages whom (never what), until the next update hides that too.

Is it on Google Play?

Not at first. Some of its protections are not allowed on Google Play, so it is installed from a file. A lighter Play edition may come later.

Is it safe to visit this website?

This site has no cookies, trackers or analytics, and loads nothing from other companies. But your browser history will still show you visited. If someone checks your devices, use a private window, and use the "Leave quickly" button (or press Esc) if you need to.
